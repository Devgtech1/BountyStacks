## Recording checklist

- Use two funded Devnet wallets (creator and contributor).
- Confirm wallet sign-in works for both wallets.
- Create a fresh bounty and retain its ID and transaction signature.
- Submit from the contributor wallet; confirm another contributor cannot read that submission.
- Accept from the creator wallet; verify the contributor receives the exact reward.
- Open Verify in a clean browser session and verify the completion record.
- Record the browser at 1080p, hide personal wallet details, keep the video under 180 seconds, and upload it to the hackathon submission form.
